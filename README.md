# PUBG PC Guide

Live site: https://pubgpc.github.io/

An independent, unofficial guide website for playing PUBG on PC: system requirements, graphics settings, FPS optimization, Steam install guides and performance tips.

## Contents

- 20 original guide articles in `guides/` (600–900 words each, with FAQ)
- 4 free in-browser tools in `tools/`:
  - FPS Settings Optimizer — recommended graphics settings for your hardware
  - System Requirements Checker — can your PC run PUBG?
  - Keybind Planner — customize and export your keybinds
  - Weapon Damage Calculator — damage, shots to kill and time to kill
- About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Disclaimer pages
- Official download links only: Steam (PC), Google Play and App Store (PUBG Mobile)
