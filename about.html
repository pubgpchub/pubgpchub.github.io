About PUBG PC Guide

PUBG PC Guide is an independent, unofficial guide website for players who want to get the most out of PUBG on PC. We write practical, tested advice about hardware, graphics settings, frame rates, Steam setup and everyday gameplay.

What We Cover

  • System requirements — what your PC actually needs to run PUBG well in 2026.
  • Graphics settings — settings tuned for high FPS and clear visibility, not just pretty screenshots.
  • Performance — fixing lag, stutter and crashes, and choosing the right hardware.
  • Steam guides — installing, updating and troubleshooting the PC version.
  • Free tools — a settings optimizer, a system checker, a keybind planner and a weapon damage calculator that run entirely in your browser.

How We Write

Every guide on this site is original writing based on hands-on PC gaming experience and publicly available specifications. We do not copy content from other sites, and we never publish cheats, hacks, aimbots or exploits — only legitimate ways to play better within the game's rules.
