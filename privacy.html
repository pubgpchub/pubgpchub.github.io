Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What This Site Collects

PUBG PC Guide is a static information website. We do not run user accounts, comments or forums, and we do not ask you for personal information to read our guides or use our tools. The tools on this site (FPS optimizer, system checker, keybind planner, weapon calculator) run entirely in your browser — the values you enter are not sent to any server.

Contact by Email

If you email us at contact@pubgpc.github.io, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that only to read and, where needed, reply to your message. We do not sell, share or publish email addresses.

Cookies and Analytics

This site does not set its own cookies and does not run its own analytics. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may collect standard server logs, such as IP addresses and requested pages, for security and operational purposes.

External Links

This site links to official third-party pages: Steam, Google Play and the Apple App Store, and PUBG Support. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. Clicking a download link takes you to the official store page for the game.

Children

This site is a general information site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@pubgpc.github.io.
