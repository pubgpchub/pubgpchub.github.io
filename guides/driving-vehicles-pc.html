Driving Vehicles in PUBG on PC: Controls, Tips & Best Vehicles

PUBG PC Guide · Updated November 2026 · 5 min read

Vehicles are the fastest way to rotate across PUBG's large maps, escape the blue zone, and reach a strong position before other squads. This guide covers the controls, handling, and smart driving habits that keep you alive on the road.

Basic Vehicle Controls on PC

Driving in PUBG on PC uses simple keyboard controls. Press F to enter or exit a vehicle, and use the number keys to switch seats. W accelerates, S brakes or reverses, and A and D steer left and right. Hold Shift to boost on most vehicles, and press Space for the handbrake, which helps you turn sharply or stop quickly. The mouse controls your camera while driving, so look around to check for enemies before you commit to a route. Passengers can shoot from most seats, while the driver usually cannot fire a weapon, so the driver should focus entirely on the road. On motorcycles, the driver can use a handgun in some situations, but that is the exception rather than the rule. Learn the controls in a quiet area at the start of a match before you need them under pressure, and check your keybinds in the settings if any of these defaults feel uncomfortable.

Handling, Terrain and Speed

Every vehicle in PUBG handles differently, and the terrain changes everything. Roads are fast and predictable, while hills, rocks, and trees can slow you down, damage your vehicle, or flip it completely. Slow down before steep hills and sharp turns, especially in heavier vehicles that are harder to control. Motorcycles are fast and nimble, but they are also the easiest to crash, and a bad landing can injure or knock out the rider. Cars and trucks are more stable but slower to turn, so plan your route early and avoid sudden steering at high speed. Watch the terrain ahead rather than the minimap while driving, and use the handbrake to slide around corners on dirt roads. If your vehicle flips, try steering and accelerating gently to recover it; if it stays upside down, leave it and move on foot rather than wasting time. Good driving is mostly about patience: arriving a few seconds later is better than crashing halfway there.

Best Vehicles for Different Situations

The best vehicle depends on what you need it for. For fast solo rotations, a motorcycle or coupe is hard to beat, because both are quick and easy to park behind cover. For a full squad, a four-seat car such as a sedan or SUV is the practical choice, since everyone can ride together and passengers can shoot from the windows. Trucks and vans are slower but tougher, which makes them useful when you expect to drive through an area where enemies may shoot at you. On water-heavy maps, boats let you cross rivers and coastlines that would otherwise force a long detour, but they leave you exposed, so use them only when the route is worth it. Fuel, Damage and Repairs

Vehicles use fuel, and running out in the open is one of the most avoidable deaths in PUBG. Check the fuel gauge on the dashboard display before a long rotation, and pick up a gas can when you see one, especially if you plan to drive across a large map. To refuel, hold the gas can and use it near the vehicle. Vehicles also take damage from bullets, collisions, and explosions. A smoking vehicle is close to exploding, so stop, get out, and take cover if you see heavy smoke or flames. Do not stay inside a burning vehicle hoping to reach safety, because the explosion can knock out everyone inside. You can repair minor damage only by switching to a fresh vehicle, since there is no general repair tool for cars in normal play. Treat every vehicle as disposable: use it to reach your position, then park it behind cover or a ridge so it does not give away your location.

Driving Safely in Fights and Rotations

Vehicles are loud, and a good squad will hear you coming long before they see you. Avoid driving straight through the centre of a town or compound, and slow down as you approach your destination so you can stop behind cover instead of in the open. When rotating late in a match, use ridges, trees, and smoke from the terrain to hide your approach, and stop early so you can move the last stretch on foot. Never drive in a straight line when enemies are shooting at you; weave gently and change direction so you are harder to hit. If you are ambushed, keep driving past the threat rather than stopping in the kill zone, and only stop once you are behind solid cover. In squad play, agree on a driver before you get in, and let passengers call out enemies, directions, and hazards. A calm driver and clear callouts turn a risky rotation into a routine one.

Safety rule: Never park your vehicle in the open next to your hiding spot. Enemies use parked vehicles as a clue that a squad is nearby. Park behind a building, rock, or ridge, and walk the last few metres.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest vehicle in PUBG on PC?

Motorcycles and coupes are among the fastest land vehicles, with motorcycles slightly quicker on open roads. Speed varies by map and terrain, so a buggy or a car may be faster off-road where a motorcycle would crash.

Can the driver shoot while driving?

In most vehicles, the driver cannot shoot and should focus on driving. Passengers can fire from their seats. Motorcycles are an exception, where the driver can sometimes use a handgun.

How do I refuel a vehicle in PUBG?

Pick up a gas can, stand near the vehicle, and use the gas can from your inventory. The fuel gauge will rise as it refuels, so top up before long rotations rather than waiting until the tank is empty.
