Best Scopes and Optics for PUBG on PC

PUBG PC Guide · Updated November 2026 · 5 min read

The right scope can turn a long-range guess into a clean, confident shot. This guide explains every optic in PUBG on PC, when to use each one, and how to set up your sights so they feel natural in real fights.

How Scopes Work in PUBG

Scopes attach to a weapon's optic slot and magnify your view when you aim down sights. Higher magnification shows distant enemies more clearly, but it narrows your field of view and makes your aim more sensitive to small mouse movements. That trade-off is the heart of choosing an optic: more zoom is not always better. Close-range fights favour speed and awareness, while open-field fights reward magnification and a steady aim. Learn each optic in training mode before relying on it in a ranked match, and check how quickly you can find a target after aiming in. A good scope should help you spot enemies faster, not slow you down.

Close-Range Optics: Red Dot and Holographic Sights

Red dot and holographic sights are the best choices for close-quarters combat. They give no magnification, which keeps your peripheral vision wide and lets you track fast-moving enemies at short range. The red dot is simple and easy to learn, with a small centre point that does not block much of your target. The holographic sight has a slightly larger frame and a circular reticle, which some players find easier to centre quickly. Both work well on assault rifles, submachine guns, and shotguns. If you fight mostly inside buildings or around compounds, a red dot is usually the safer pick. Try both for a few matches and keep the one that feels more comfortable.

Mid-Range Scopes: 2x, 3x and 4x

Mid-range scopes cover the distances where most PUBG fights actually happen. A 2x scope is a strong all-rounder: it gives enough zoom to hit targets at 100 to 200 metres while still being usable in closer fights. The 3x scope is popular on assault rifles and designated marksman rifles because it balances zoom and handling well. A 4x scope suits longer engagements and works especially well on marksman rifles, though it can feel slow if an enemy rushes you. Use these scopes for fights across open ground or between compounds. Remember that a mid-range scope still needs good recoil control, so practise burst firing and single shots at distance rather than holding the trigger down.

Long-Range Scopes: 6x and 8x

The 6x and 8x scopes are built for sniping and long-range marksman play. An 8x scope gives the clearest view of distant targets and pairs naturally with bolt-action sniper rifles, while a 6x scope is more flexible because you can switch it down to 3x magnification with the zoom key. That adjustable zoom makes the 6x one of the most useful scopes in the game, since it can handle both medium and very long shots. Long-range scopes demand patience and a stable position: find cover, go prone or crouch, and take your time with each shot. They are poor choices for close fights, so always carry a second weapon with a red dot or iron sights for emergencies. If you play as your squad's sniper, a 6x or 8x scope is worth looting for, but do not pick one up just because it is rare. It only helps if your weapon and your playstyle suit long-range shooting.

Zeroing, Sensitivity and Practical Tips

Zeroing adjusts the distance your scope is set for, which matters at long range because bullets drop as they travel. Use Page Up and Page Down by default to raise or lower it, and match it roughly to your target's distance when shooting beyond 200 metres. At shorter ranges, the default 100-metre zero is usually fine. Your scoped sensitivity also matters: if your aim feels too fast or too slow when scoped in, adjust the per-scope sensitivity settings until each magnification feels consistent. Keep a red dot on your secondary weapon and a magnified scope on your primary, so you are ready for both close and long fights. Finally, practise in training mode at 100, 200, and 300 metres to learn how much your bullets drop at each distance.

Quick tip: Do not hold a 6x or 8x scope on a moving target for too long. Aim, breathe, take the shot, and reposition. A scoped-in player is easy to spot, and a missed first shot tells the enemy exactly where you are.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best scope for beginners in PUBG on PC?

A red dot sight is the best starting point, with a 2x scope as a good second choice. Both are easy to learn, work at the ranges where most fights happen, and do not punish small aiming mistakes the way a 6x or 8x scope can.

Is the 6x scope better than the 8x scope?

For most players, yes. The 6x scope can switch between 3x and 6x magnification, so it handles medium and long range. The 8x scope gives a clearer long-range view, but it is only useful on sniper rifles and is poor in close fights.

How do I change zeroing distance in PUBG on PC?

Use Page Up to increase and Page Down to decrease the zeroing distance while aiming down sights. Match it to your target's distance for shots beyond 200 metres, and leave it at 100 metres for closer fights.
