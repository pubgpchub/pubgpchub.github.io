How to Download and Install PUBG on Steam (PC)

PUBG PC Guide · Updated November 2026 · 5 min read

PUBG: Battlegrounds is free to play on PC through Steam, and getting it installed takes only a few steps. You need a Steam account, enough free storage, and a stable internet connection for the download. This guide walks you through the whole process, from creating an account to launching your first match.

What You Need Before You Start

Before you download anything, check that your PC meets the minimum system requirements and that you have enough storage. PUBG needs around 40 to 50 GB of free space, plus extra room for updates, so aim for at least 60 GB free on the drive where you plan to install it. An SSD is strongly recommended for faster loading. You will also need a stable broadband connection, because the download is large and a dropped connection can slow things down. Finally, make sure Windows is up to date and your graphics drivers are current, since outdated drivers are a common cause of crashes on first launch.

Step 1: Create a Steam Account

If you do not already have Steam, go to the official Steam website at store.steampowered.com and click Join Steam. Enter your email address, choose an account name and a strong password, and confirm the verification email Steam sends you. Pick an account name you are happy to keep, because Steam does not let you change it later. Once your account is created, turn on Steam Guard, the two-factor protection in your account settings, so your account stays safe. If you already have a Steam account, skip this step and sign in with your existing details.

Step 2: Download and Install the Steam Client

From the Steam website, click Install Steam to download the setup file, then run it and follow the installer. Accept the default install location unless you have a reason to change it, and let Steam finish its first update after launching. Sign in with the account you just created. The Steam client is the store, library and launcher in one, and PUBG can only be installed through it on PC, so keep it installed even after the game is downloaded. You can set Steam to start with Windows or open it manually each time; either way works.

Step 3: Find PUBG and Start the Download

  1. Open Steam and use the search box at the top to search for PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS.
  2. Open the game's store page and check that it is the free-to-play version published by KRAFTON.
  3. Click the green Play Game button, or Add to Library if you want to install it later.
  4. Choose the drive where you want to install it, preferably an SSD with at least 60 GB free.
  5. Accept the terms and click Next to start the download.
  6. Wait for the download to finish. You can watch the progress in the Downloads section at the bottom of the Steam window.

The download size is large, so the time it takes depends on your internet speed. On a 50 Mbps connection, expect around two hours; on a faster connection, much less. You can pause and resume the download at any time, and you can keep using your PC for other things while it runs. Avoid shutting down your computer mid-download, though Steam will simply resume from where it stopped.

Step 4: First Launch and Setup

When the download finishes, the game appears in your Steam Library. Click Play to launch it for the first time. PUBG may install additional components such as DirectX and the Visual C++ redistributables on this first run; let them finish. The game will then ask you to accept the terms of service and create your in-game nickname. Choose your nickname carefully, because changing it later may cost in-game currency. After that, the game will download a small additional update or shader cache, then take you to the main lobby. Before your first match, open Settings and set your graphics options to match your hardware, and spend ten minutes in Training Mode to test your controls and frame rate.

Tip: Only download PUBG from the official Steam store page. Websites offering "free PUBG installers" or cracked copies are not legitimate, and they often contain malware or lead to banned accounts. Steam is the only official way to play PUBG on PC.

Fixing Common Install Problems

If the download is stuck or very slow, first check your Steam download region in Settings, then Downloads, and pick the server closest to your location. If the game crashes on launch, verify the game files by right-clicking PUBG in your Library, choosing Properties, then Installed Files, then Verify integrity of game files. If you see a disk write error, run Steam as an administrator and make sure your antivirus is not blocking the Steam folders. If the game says you do not have enough space, check that you selected the right drive during installation, and free up room if needed. Most install problems are fixed by one of these steps, and Steam's own support pages cover the rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PUBG free on PC?

Yes. PUBG: Battlegrounds switched to free-to-play on PC in January 2022. You can download and play it at no cost through Steam, with optional paid items in the in-game store.

How much storage space does PUBG need on PC?

The game needs around 40 to 50 GB of free space, and you should keep at least 60 GB free on the drive to leave room for updates. Installing on an SSD is strongly recommended for faster loading.

Do I need a Steam account to play PUBG on PC?

Yes. On PC, PUBG is distributed through Steam, so you need a free Steam account and the Steam client to download, install and launch the game.

Download PUBG Now
