Contact Us

We welcome corrections, suggestions and guide requests. If you found an error in one of our guides, or there is a PUBG PC topic you would like us to cover, let us know.

Email: contact@pubgpc.github.io

Please include the guide title and, if it is a correction, the section where the error appears. We read every message, though we cannot reply to all of them.

What We Can Help With

Corrections to our guides, suggestions for new guides or tools, and general feedback about the site. For help with the game itself — account issues, bans, purchases or in-game bugs — please contact official PUBG Support, as we are an independent fan guide and cannot access player accounts.

What We Cannot Do

We do not provide cheats, hacks, aimbots or any tool that breaks the game's rules, and we do not sell or distribute game files. The only download links on this site are the official Steam, Google Play and App Store pages.
