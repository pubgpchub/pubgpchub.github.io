Weapon Damage Calculator

Pick a weapon, your target's armor and the distance. The calculator works out body-shot and headshot damage, shots to kill and time to kill.

Damage values are approximate community-tested figures and may change with game updates. Armor reduces damage: Level 1 blocks 30%, Level 2 blocks 40%, Level 3 blocks 55%. Headshots deal roughly double damage before helmet reduction. Learn how to use each weapon class in our scopes and optics guide.
